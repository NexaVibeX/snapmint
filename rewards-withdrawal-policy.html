Rewards & Withdrawal Policy

This policy explains the general rules for SnapMint rewards and eligible withdrawals.

Rewards

Rewards may be generated through eligible activities displayed in the app, such as qualifying engagement, campaigns, daily activities or advertisements. The exact rates, limits and eligibility are controlled by the applicable program configuration shown in the app.

Eligibility

Users must meet the eligibility requirements displayed by SnapMint. Accounts involved in manipulation, fraud, duplicate activity or other prohibited behavior may be excluded.

Withdrawal

Eligible users may submit withdrawal requests using supported UPI or bank methods. Minimum withdrawal amounts, verification requirements and processing times may be displayed in the withdrawal screen and can change.

Verification

SnapMint may request information necessary to validate an account or prevent fraud before processing a withdrawal.

Review

Transactions may be reviewed before completion. Suspicious or invalid rewards may be adjusted or cancelled in accordance with the applicable program rules and law.

Important

Rewards are not a guaranteed income and should not be represented as guaranteed earnings. Availability depends on the applicable program, account eligibility and platform rules.
