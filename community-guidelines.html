Community Guidelines

SnapMint is built for creative expression, discovery and respectful interaction. These guidelines apply to posts, reels, stories, comments, profiles, messages and challenge submissions.

Allowed

  • Original creative content.
  • Respectful opinions and discussions.
  • Educational and entertainment content that follows applicable law.

Not Allowed

  • Child sexual exploitation or sexual content involving minors.
  • Credible threats, targeted harassment or violent encouragement.
  • Hate or dehumanizing attacks against protected groups.
  • Fraud, scams, impersonation and deceptive schemes.
  • Spam, fake engagement or manipulation of rankings.
  • Malware, phishing or harmful links.
  • Content that infringes intellectual property rights.
  • Illegal goods or activities.

Reporting and Blocking

Users can report or block accounts through available in-app controls. Reports may be reviewed and may result in content removal or account restrictions.

Enforcement

Depending on severity and history, SnapMint may warn, limit, remove content, restrict features, suspend or terminate accounts.
