Account & Data Deletion

SnapMint provides a way for users to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Deletion

Use the account deletion option available in SnapMint, if provided, or submit a deletion request through the official support/contact method.

What May Be Deleted

  • Account/profile information, subject to legal exceptions.
  • User-generated content where deletion is technically and legally applicable.
  • Associated preferences and identifiers.

What May Be Retained

Certain records may be retained where required for legal compliance, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, accounting or enforcement of agreements.

Processing

Deletion requests may require account verification. Processing time can vary depending on the data and applicable requirements.
