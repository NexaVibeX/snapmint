SnapMint (“SnapMint”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) provides a social and creator platform where users can create, share, discover and interact with content. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, shared and protected when you use SnapMint.

We may collect content you choose to upload, including photos, videos, stories, posts, captions and profile media. We may also collect engagement information such as likes, comments, views, follows and challenge participation.

When you use features that require them, the app may request access to camera, photos/media or notifications.

2. How We Use Information

  • Display and personalize social content.

  • Provide analytics and improve app performance.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, spam and security incidents.

3. Service Providers

SnapMint may use third-party services such as Firebase for authentication, database, analytics and messaging; Cloudinary for media storage/delivery; and Google AdMob for advertising. These providers may process information according to their own policies and applicable agreements.

4. User Content

Content you publish may be visible to other users depending on the feature and account settings. Do not upload content or personal information that you do not have the right to share.

5. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements and prevent abuse.

7. Account and Data Deletion

You may request account/data deletion through the in-app account controls or the designated support/contact method. Some information may be retained where required for legal, security or fraud-prevention purposes.

8. Children

SnapMint is not intended for children who are below the minimum age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

For privacy questions or requests, contact SnapMint through the support/contact method provided in the app or on the official SnapMint website.
